## About VETO

**VETO: New Era Roleplay** is a FiveM roleplay project set in a fictional nation caught in the middle of political instability, territorial conflicts, and international military intervention.

Built around a unique geopolitical storyline, VETO offers a roleplay environment where military operations, civilian life, political interests, and criminal organizations coexist in an evolving world.

Every character has a story. Every decision has consequences. Every operation can change the future of the nation.

## The Story of VETO

Veto is a small nation strategically located between major international trade routes and resource-rich territories.

Despite its relatively small size, the country possesses extraordinary natural wealth, including:

- Petroleum and crude oil.
- Natural gas reserves.
- Rare and strategic minerals.
- Uranium deposits.

For years, Veto maintained relatively stable political and economic conditions. However, internal corruption, political instability, and weakening national security gradually undermined the government's authority.

Armed groups began infiltrating the country's border regions, initially engaging in illegal arms trafficking and black-market operations.

Over time, these groups expanded their influence, established military capabilities, seized strategic facilities, and took control of valuable natural resources.

Several regions eventually fell into armed conflict.

**The government of Veto could no longer maintain control over its entire territory.**

Facing an increasingly critical situation, the Veto government formally requested military assistance from the United States.

Following bilateral negotiations, the United States deployed military forces to Veto with the objectives of:

- Reclaiming territories controlled by armed factions.
- Securing strategic infrastructure and natural resources.
- Protecting civilians from terrorist threats.
- Restoring national security and political stability.

However, military intervention did not immediately end the conflict.

Armed factions continued to resist, political tensions escalated, and the future of Veto became increasingly uncertain.

**Veto had become an international military operational zone.**

And this is where our story begins.

## Roleplay Environment

VETO is designed to deliver a dynamic roleplay experience built around several interconnected aspects of the fictional nation.

### Military Operations

Experience military-oriented roleplay involving deployments, tactical operations, territorial security, and conflict resolution.

### Government & Politics

Take part in the political landscape of Veto, where government institutions, national interests, and international relations shape the country's future.

### Law Enforcement

Maintain public order, investigate criminal activities, secure important locations, and protect civilians.

### Civilian Life

Not everyone in Veto carries a weapon. Civilians, workers, entrepreneurs, and ordinary citizens all contribute to the nation's living world.

### Armed Factions

Operate within the unstable regions of Veto, where armed organizations, underground networks, and competing interests influence the ongoing conflict.

## Our Vision

Our goal is to build more than just another FiveM server.

We aim to create a structured and immersive roleplay ecosystem where:

- Storytelling drives the experience.
- Player decisions influence the world.
- Factions have meaningful roles and responsibilities.
- Military and civilian roleplay remain interconnected.
- Every operation contributes to an evolving narrative.
- Community participation helps shape the future of Veto.

We believe that great roleplay comes from meaningful interactions, consistent storytelling, and a community that builds the world together.
